Hillside Villa An

A family villa stepped down a pine-covered slope in Da Lat, opening every living space to the valley through full-height glazing and warm timber joinery.

The brief

The clients wanted a weekend house that would eventually become a retirement home — low-maintenance, single-level living where possible, and framed views of the pine hills from every main room. The site falls four metres from road to garden, which became the organizing idea: living spaces terraced along the contour.

The concept

We kept the palette close to the landscape — local pine ceilings, terrazzo floors poured on site, and deep eaves that shade the glass through the hot afternoons. Furniture was designed as a family of pieces: a modular sofa, a dining table in solid ash, and joinery wall that conceals services and storage so the rooms stay visually quiet.

  • Living and dining open fully to the terrace through sliding glass walls
  • The kitchen sits half a level below, keeping it part of the gathering without dominating it
  • Bedrooms face east to catch morning light over the valley

The outcome

Completed over fourteen months, the house now hosts three generations most weekends. The clients’ only brief for the furniture — “nothing that looks fragile” — shaped every detail, from the 60 mm solid table top to the woven-leather bench at the entry.
