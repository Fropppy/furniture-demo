Atelier 24 Showroom

A furniture showroom in a converted printworks — 380 m² of raw shell, rational steel racks and our workshop's pieces displayed like sculpture.

The brief

Atelier 24 manufactures furniture and needed a showroom that doubles as an event space for architects and designers. Products had to be touchable, changeable, and legible from the street.

The concept

We kept the shell raw — painted brick, exposed services, polished screed — and introduced one precise element: a system of black steel racks and oak platforms that displays pieces at eye level and rebuilds for each collection. A 6 m pivot door opens the corner fully on event nights.

  • Mobile platforms allow the floor to be cleared for talks of eighty people
  • Material library wall holds our full palette of timbers, stones and fabrics
  • The mezzanine workshop window lets visitors watch pieces being finished

The outcome

The showroom now hosts a monthly designer talk series that keeps the brand in the city’s conversation. Product dwell time doubled against their previous shop-in-shop.
