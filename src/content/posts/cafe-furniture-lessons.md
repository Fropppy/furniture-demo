After four decades' worth of café fit-outs between our teams, patterns emerge. The tables that survive are never the ones that photograph best — they are the ones with steel protection at knee height, replaceable foot glides and tops that can be sanded, not repainted.

We now standardise the invisible 20% of every café we build: hardware, fixings, edge details and maintenance clearances. The visible 80% stays unique to the brand. Clients get durability without a template look, and our workshop cuts waste by batch-producing the hidden parts.
