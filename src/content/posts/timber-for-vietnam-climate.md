Vietnam's humidity swings are brutal on timber. Movement is not a defect to eliminate but a behaviour to design for — floating panels, elongated fixings holes, and finishes that slow moisture exchange rather than trying to seal it.

Our default specification: kiln-dried ash or oak for visible joinery, powder-coated steel where spans demand it, and good-grade plywood for carcases that will never see standing water. Solid teak earns its premium outdoors; almost nowhere else.
