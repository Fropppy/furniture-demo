## The brief

A family plot two streets from the old town, to be converted into an eleven-room homestay. The owners live on site, so the property had to work as both a small business and a home — with construction that respected the neighbourhood's scale and heritage guidelines.

## The concept

Rooms wrap a courtyard kept cool by an existing palm and a reflecting pool. Lime plaster on brick, handmade tiles and local dark timber keep the building unmistakably of Hoi An, while the furniture — four-poster beds, writing desks, louvered shutters — was designed as a repeatable kit the family's carpenters can maintain.

- Every room opens to the courtyard through private verandas
- Breakfast is served at a single long communal table under the palm
- Rainwater from the roof irrigates the garden

## The outcome

The homestay holds a 9.4 rating two years in, with guests repeatedly citing the courtyard. Occupancy averages 85% — the family has since commissioned the adjacent plot's design from us.
