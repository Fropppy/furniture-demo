## The brief

An agency of sixty people moving from a generic tower floor into a warehouse. They wanted the building's character kept — trusses, brick, height — with workplaces that support deep focus as well as their very social studio culture.

## The concept

Programme is zoned by noise: social and meeting at the entrance, focus work toward the quiet rear. Birch plywood pods house meetings without touching the original structure. Desks are our own design — solid oak tops on steel frames, wired through the legs so no cables cross the floor.

- Twelve acoustically treated pods and phone booths
- A 9 m communal table that seats the whole studio for town halls
- Kitchen and café bar positioned to pull people through the space daily

## The outcome

The office opened as the agency's recruiting showcase. Two years of fit-out churn were avoided by designing the desks and storage for reconfiguration — the client has already rearranged twice without calling us back, which we take as a compliment.
