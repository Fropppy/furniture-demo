## The brief

A cocktail group opening their third venue wanted a room that photographs well without feeling like a set — mature, dim, and built around a bar that seats twenty.

## The concept

We treated light as the primary material. Every surface is either dark timber, unlacquered brass or deep upholstery, and the lighting scheme runs three layers deep — task light the guests never notices, glow at the bottles, and candle-scale light at the seats. Acoustic plaster above keeps conversation possible at full house.

- The 12 m walnut bar with a brass footrail was dry-fitted in our workshop first
- Booth backs are high enough to shelter tables but sight lines stay open to the bar
- Back-bar shelving is lit at 2700K to make the bottle wall the room's centrepiece

## The outcome

Luna reached its revenue target in month four. The bar counter — built to survive a decade of elbows — has already hosted a thousand first dates and an uncountable number of old fashioneds.
