## The brief

Two floors, a serious art collection, and a request for "rooms that disappear." The couple entertains monthly and wanted open flow without theecho of an empty gallery — warmth had to come from materials, not clutter.

## The concept

We built the plan around sight lines: from the entry, every axis ends at either art or window. Storage is concentrated in two full-height joinery walls finished flush to the plaster, so daily life folds away. Upholstery in bouclé and smoked oak adds the softness the concrete shell lacks.

- A 4.2 m limestone dining table anchors the double-height space
- Motorised shades and hidden cove lighting protect the artworks
- The study mezzanine doubles as a guest room with a murphy bed

## The outcome

Delivered in nine months. The couple's verdict after their first party: guests asked whether the penthouse predated the building — which, for a design team, is the right kind of confusion.
