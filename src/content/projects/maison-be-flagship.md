## The brief

Maison Be was moving from multi-brand consignment to its own flagship. The brief: a store that makes a small Vietnamese label feel international, flexible enough to re-hang the entire collection overnight, and warm enough that people stay.

## The concept

We borrowed the grammar of a gallery: a rhythmic arcade of arched niches carries the hanging system, plinths display hero pieces, and lighting is tuned to a neutral 3500K so fabric colour reads true. Every rail, shelf and mirror is on a modular grid — staff re-merchandise without tools.

- Custom hanging rails machined in brushed stainless, rated for denim walls
- Fitting rooms scaled generously with dedicated stylist chairs
- A curved bench in shearling anchors the centre of the floor

## The outcome

Opening-week sales beat projection by 40%. The niche system has since been adopted as the brand's standard for future stores — we're now detailing store number two.
