What we do

From first sketch to final styling

01

Interior architecture

Concept to construction drawings — plans, lighting, materials and joinery detail resolved before anything is built.

02

Furniture & joinery

Bespoke pieces designed, prototyped and produced in our partner workshop, from a single table to full casegoods.

03

Design–build fit-out

Turnkey delivery with one team responsible for design intent, cost and schedule through to handover.

04

Feasibility & consulting

Layout, budget and concept testing for investors before a lease is signed — so decisions are made on numbers.

How we work

A process built for trust

Most of our clients are investing in a space for the first time. The process is transparent on purpose — you always know what happens next, what it costs, and who is accountable.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Site survey, listening, and a brief that records what success means for your project.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept, then developed design — 3D views, material boards and a priced furniture list.

  3. 03

    Build

    Technical drawings, workshop production and on-site supervision by our own team.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Styling, snagging and a maintenance file. We stay reachable after the ribbon is cut.
