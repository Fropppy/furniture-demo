Studio notes — setting up our material library

A material library rots quickly if nothing is thrown away. Ours follows three rules: every sample carries a supplier, a price band and a project it was used on; anything unused for two years leaves the shelf; and samples must be the real finish, never a print.

The digital mirror of the library — photos, datasheets, care instructions — is what the whole team actually uses day to day. The physical shelf exists to force us to touch the materials before we promise them to a client.
